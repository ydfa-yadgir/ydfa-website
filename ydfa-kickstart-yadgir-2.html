Vision & Mission

Why This Project, Why Yadgir

Yadgir is one of Karnataka's aspirational districts — talent is not the gap, opportunity is.

Vision

To make Yadgir a model district where football becomes a tool for education, health, leadership, gender equality, and youth development.

Mission

To provide every child in Yadgir with access to quality football coaching, life skills education, safe play spaces, and competitive opportunities — through partnerships with schools, communities, and local institutions.

School Attendance Confidence Leadership Physical Health Gender Inclusion Youth Engagement Community Participation

KickStart Yadgir

Football for Every Child. Leadership for Every Community.

Five Pillars

How KickStart Yadgir Works

Every school, village event, girls' festival and coach workshop runs under one unified program.

01

Grassroots Football

School sessions, village coaching centres, weekend festivals and community football events.

Target: 5,000 children
02

Life Skills Through Football

Respect, empathy, leadership, communication, teamwork, goal setting and gender equality — in every session.

03

School Football Development

Every partner school gets a football club, student leaders, a school team, equipment and an annual tournament.

04

Coach Development

Monthly workshops, mentoring, practical coaching and youth leadership training for local coaches.

Target: 100 coaches
05

Competition Pathway

School League, Village League, Girls League, Community Cup, District Championship and Elite Team Trials.

Girls Football Strategy

Every cluster gets at least one girls' football centre — parent awareness meetings, girls-only coaching, female coaches where possible, a girls' district festival, and leadership workshops.
